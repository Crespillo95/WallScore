# WallScore • recording script

Target running time: 75 seconds. Capture the actual SPECS Interactive Preview in Lens Studio once all interactions below work. Keep the cursor and the response visible together. Capture continuous action for the spacing → lock → mark sequence. The printable companion can appear briefly after the native sequence.

| Time | Action on screen | English narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 s | Start at the sample wall. Hold on the three different frame shapes. | “Three pictures, three different sizes, one empty wall. I want to see the arrangement before making a single mark.” |
| 10–24 s | Step sideways and return. Point at the left frame and its neighbour. | “WallScore places their outlines at their actual sizes. Their centres share a level line. The gap is measured between edges, even when the frames have different widths.” |
| 24–39 s | Change the gap from 8 to 12 cm, then back to 8 cm. Hold after each change. | “A single pinch gives the pictures more breathing room. I can compare the two arrangements while looking straight at the wall.” |
| 39–56 s | Choose Show marks. Frame the middle mark and read the two dimensions. | “This landscape frame hangs three centimetres below its top edge. The guide puts its hook 123 centimetres from the left edge and 162 above the floor.” |
| 56–67 s | Step closer, then return to layout. Show that the gap is still 8 cm. | “Each frame has its own hanger height. The three hooks can sit at different heights while the pictures still look level.” |
| 67–75 s | Briefly show the printable mounting guide, then end on the native wall. | “This demo uses a simulated wall. At the real wall, check the measurements with a tape before fixing the hooks.” |
