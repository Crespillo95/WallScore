# WallScore

**Tagline**  
Plan three pictures at full scale. Find the exact height of each hanger before marking the wall.

**Who it is for**  
Someone hanging a small exhibition or a group of pictures at home. With three different frame sizes, aligning their centres leaves the hooks at different heights. A fixed distance between the hooks also gives uneven gaps between the pictures.

**Design**  
WallScore uses a shared centre line and equal spacing between frame edges. Its planned SPECS interaction places the three outlines on a sample wall, lets the installer compare gaps with a pinch and reveals each hanger position on demand. The installer can keep looking at the arrangement while holding a picture or a measuring tape. The accompanying mounting sheet provides the measurements in English or Spanish.

**What the prototype calculations demonstrate**  
For the example 240 cm wall and 8 cm gaps, the three hooks sit 80, 123 and 163 cm from the left edge. Their heights above the floor are 166, 162 and 163 cm. All three pictures end up centred at 150 cm. Changing the gap recalculates both margins and every hook location.

**How it was developed**  
The entrant selected a mounting guide and delegated the implementation details. Codex produced the interaction specification, original frame meshes, guide, controller, local tests, narration draft and closing video shot. The project starts from the SPECS Base Template. The printable guide and controller share the same geometry module.
